The Chase

You bat, the AI bowls pace and spin, sets the field and learns where you score.

It plays best on a computer. Timing your shot is much easier with a keyboard or a game controller, and the ground is small on a phone screen.

  • On a phone, turn it sideways. There’s an on-screen pad for shots.
  • A Bluetooth game controller works on phones and tablets too.
